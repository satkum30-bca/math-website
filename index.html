The Fibonacci Sequence

You've probably heard of the Fibonacci sequence before. It is defined with a simple rule: adding the last two numbers to get the next. At the same time, it pops up everywhere in nature and generates many interesting mathematical properties. This website serves as a short introduction to the theory behind the Fibonacci sequence, reframing the sequence in terms of linear algebra to derive many core results.

The first section introduces the Fibonacci-Q matrix, which generates terms of the Fibonacci sequence via exponentiation. From here, we diagonalize this matrix to derive Binet's formula, a direct formula for the nth term of the Fibonacci Sequence. Finally, we use the matrix to find identities regarding the Fibonacci sequence such as Cassini's identity. There are also visualizations and exercises to make learning easier.
